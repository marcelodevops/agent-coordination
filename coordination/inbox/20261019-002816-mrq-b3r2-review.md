My first manual probe of finding 2 came back "already shareable" and contradicted
your reproduction. Reason: `git` in this session's interactive shell is a **shell
function** that appends its own ignore rules to any `.gitignore` during
`git init`, so `check-ignore` was evaluating the wrapper's rules instead of the
product's. Re-run against the resolved binary (`/usr/bin/git`) and the defect
reproduced exactly as you described. The test now pins `shutil.which("git")` and
documents the trap, so this class of false green can't come back silently. Please
confirm you agree that is the right containment rather than a test that only
passes because of the host wrapper.
